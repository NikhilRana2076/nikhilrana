03 — Featured research · MSc dissertation 2026

Can AI be trusted with company accounts?

Detecting and mitigating LLM hallucinations in UK iXBRL financial reporting

UK accounts are filed in iXBRL, where each figure carries a machine-readable tag. I built a pipeline that parses those tags into a fact store and retrieves by tag, then tested it against a 102-question benchmark on 12 real filings from companies including Tesco, Lloyds, AstraZeneca and National Grid.

Supervised by Dr Ali Gheitasy · Claude Sonnet 4.6 & GPT-5.6 Terra · Python, lxml, SQLite, pandas

A live demo is in progress. You upload any UK filing, browse its tagged facts and question it with Claude or GPT. Until it’s public, I’m happy to walk you through it.

Limitations I report openly

  • 12 filings and 102 questions, reduced from the planned 50 and ~500 with supervisor approval.
  • The baseline was cost-constrained to 8,000 tokens of context.
  • Reporting framework and company size are confounded in the corpus.
  • The planned independent dual review couldn’t be completed.

Where I’d take it next

  • Scale towards 50 filings and 500 questions.
  • Build a detector that checks against all legitimate tagged values.
  • Test a finance-specialised model such as FinGPT or FinMA.
  • Ship the public demo.
