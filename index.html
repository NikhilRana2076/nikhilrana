About

I am an MSc Artificial Intelligence graduate from the University of West London with a quantitative background in statistics, probability and simulation from my BSc in Computer Science & Information Technology.

My dissertation examined why large language models hallucinate on structured financial data. I built a complete retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline over UK Companies House iXBRL filings and showed that tag-aware retrieval improves answer accuracy from 44.1% to 92.2% (p < 0.001). I also took part in the Financial Reporting Council (FRC) Hackathon, which gave me direct experience with regulatory financial data.

Before my master’s, I worked as a Data Analyst Intern at WorldLink Communications, building customer-segmentation and churn-risk models that informed the marketing team’s retention strategy. I value careful measurement, reproducible work and clear communication of results to non-technical stakeholders.
